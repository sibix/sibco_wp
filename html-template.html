Quality Is Not an Act — It's a Process at Every Stage

At SIBCO, quality assurance begins before production starts. Every batch of raw coir fibre is tested for critical parameters including fibre length, tensile strength, moisture content, and cleanliness.

During production, in-line quality checks monitor weave density, dimensional accuracy, rubber backing adhesion, and embossing depth. Our quality team conducts random sampling at defined intervals to ensure process consistency.

Final inspection covers every aspect — dimensions, weight, visual appearance, backing integrity, and packaging compliance. Only products that pass all checkpoints are approved for export dispatch.
